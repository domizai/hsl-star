Colour solid in CIELAB

The coloured body is every colour HSL* can produce, which is the sRGB gamut. Height is L*, the floor is a* and b*. The outline is the optimal colour solid: all surface colours that are physically possible under D65. Drag to rotate, double-click to reset.
